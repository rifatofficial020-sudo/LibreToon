# Tutorials

## Edit the Motion Tween example

1. Open **Home → Examples → Motion Tween**. LibreToon opens an editable copy.
2. Select a tweened object and scrub between the keys to see the original motion.
3. Change the easing in **Properties** and compare how quickly the object leaves and approaches its keys.
4. Save the exercise under a new name.

[Download the Motion Tween project](https://github.com/alvillafm/LibreToon/raw/refs/heads/main/assets/examples/MotionTween_Example.ltoon).

## Draw a breakdown with Shift & Trace

1. Draw two poses with a blank keyframe between them.
2. Move to the blank key and turn on **Shift & Trace** in the timeline.
3. Choose Reference A or B and use Selection to move its overlay. Drag its handles to rotate or scale it.
4. Switch to a brush and draw the breakdown. Turn off **Shift & Trace** to restore the overlays' original alignment.
